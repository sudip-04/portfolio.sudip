Emerging
design identity

I used to think of design mainly as the appearance and layout of a building. Through my first year of study, that view has started to change.

I now see design as a process that connects people, technology, materials, structure and the way a building is constructed and used. My background in science and computer studies gives me a logical and problem-solving approach, but construction and design have shown me that there is not always one correct answer.

Working with AutoCAD and Revit has changed how I understand buildings. Instead of seeing a drawing as a final image, I am beginning to see it as a representation of systems: load paths, materials, spaces, levels, connections and decisions that affect how a building can actually be delivered.

The area I am most curious about is the relationship between design and construction. I want to understand how digital models, technical knowledge and project coordination can turn an idea into something buildable, efficient and responsible.

Learning &
skills snapshot

My confidence has grown most when I can connect a digital representation with the physical logic behind it. My next development areas are advanced Revit/BIM, technical documentation, estimating, procurement and the construction processes that sit behind a project.

03 / 07

Discipline
direction

My current direction is Building and Construction Management, with a growing interest in project coordination, BIM and digital construction.

I am interested in the part of a project where different disciplines have to work together. Construction management appeals to me because it combines technical understanding with communication, planning, sequencing, cost awareness and decision-making.

I also want to keep exploring related areas such as quantity surveying, procurement and contract administration. These areas could strengthen my understanding of how projects move from drawings and concepts into budgets, agreements, procurement decisions and construction.

I do not need to have every part of my future career decided yet. I need to build enough knowledge to make better decisions about it.

This uncertainty is useful. My goal is to develop a broad technical foundation first, then identify the combination of construction, digital tools and management where I can contribute most effectively.
